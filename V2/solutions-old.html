QMTS SOLUTIONS

This system allows several remote devices or hosts to securely communicate as though they are connected by a single IP network with uncomplicated routing. This is achieved through a central hub, the Quick Route Managing Service (QRMS), to which all clients connect.

In cases where a device has more than one connection, the QRMS also evaluates and aggregates individual link quality to maximise throughput and availability. Connections to the QRMS can be made over any public or private TCP/IP network and are insensitive to intermediate NAT and firewall operations.

For security, the QRMS link protocol also encrypts all data using link-unique random keys agreed on a per-connection basis through Diffie-Hellman exchange (ephemeral keying). Optional authentication of both the client and QRMS can be performed by exchanging cryptographic hashes which incorporate pre-shared key material.

Quad Modem Telemetry System (QMTS) devices are one type of QRMS client and are designed to utilise up to 4 wireless modems to provide exceptional link reliability and throughput when connecting to the central QRMS.

  • Input Voltage Range 9V to 28V DC
  • Nominal Current Draw 1A @ 12V
  • Operational Modulation Up to 4 Cellular Modules
  • Gigabit Interfaces 2x RJ45 Ruggedized Ethernet
  • Telemetry Array GPS & Wi-Fi Tracking Transceivers
